## What is *not* claimed

- S.2154 is **not Sanskrit**.
- No attested Sanskrit title is registered here.
- A Sanskrit back-translation of 甚深大迴向經 is not evidence for an Indic manuscript.
- Doctrinal similarity to Prajñāpāramitā or Akṣobhya literature is not the same as a direct textual parallel.
